---
title: "Stripe Setup Guide"
description: "Step-by-step instructions to connect your Stripe account to Indic8 in 2 minutes."
---

Follow this guide to connect Stripe. You will copy an endpoint URL from Indic8 into your Stripe Dashboard and copy back a webhook signing secret.

## What You Need

| Item | Where to get it | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indic8 Webhook URL** | Indic8 Dashboard > Settings > Integrations > Stripe | Tells Stripe where to send payment notifications |
| **Stripe Signing Secret** | Stripe Dashboard > Developers > Webhooks | Verifies that incoming payment data is genuine |
| **Stripe Restricted Key** *(Optional)* | Stripe Dashboard > Developers > API Keys | Imports historical past months data |

---

## Step 1: Get your Webhook URL from Indic8

1. Open your [Indic8 Dashboard](https://app.indic8.ing).
2. Go to **Settings > Integrations > Stripe**.
3. Click **Copy** next to your unique Webhook URL. It looks like:
   ```text
   https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/stripe/whk_live_xxxxxxxx
   ```

---

## Step 2: Add Webhook in Stripe Dashboard

1. Open your [Stripe Dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com).
2. In the top right navigation, click **Developers**.
3. In the left menu, click **Webhooks**.
4. Click the blue button **Add destination** (or **Add an endpoint**).
5. In the **Endpoint URL** box, paste the URL you copied from Indic8 in Step 1.
6. Under **Description**, you can enter `Indic8 Revenue Telemetry`.

---

## Step 3: Select Required Webhook Events

Click **Select events** and check the boxes for these 9 events:

| Event Group | Event Name | What it updates in Indic8 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Checkout** | `checkout.session.completed` | New customer purchases and checkouts |
| **Charges** | `charge.succeeded` | Successful card payments |
| **Charges** | `charge.refunded` | Customer refunds (deducted from net revenue) |
| **Charges** | `charge.dispute.created` | Payment chargebacks and disputes |
| **Subscriptions** | `customer.subscription.created` | New recurring monthly/yearly subscribers |
| **Subscriptions** | `customer.subscription.updated` | Plan upgrades or downgrades |
| **Subscriptions** | `customer.subscription.deleted` | Canceled subscriptions (churn tracking) |
| **Invoices** | `invoice.paid` | Recurring subscription payments and B2B invoices |
| **Invoices** | `invoice.payment_failed` | Failed renewal payments |

Click **Add events** at the bottom of the dialog, then click **Add endpoint** to save.

---

## Step 4: Copy Signing Secret into Indic8

1. On the new webhook page you just created in Stripe, look for **Signing secret** on the right side.
2. Click **Reveal**.
3. Copy the secret key (it begins with `whsec_...`).
4. Go back to your Indic8 tab, paste this string into the **Signing Secret** input box, and click **Save & Connect**.

---

## Step 5: Verify the Connection

1. In your Stripe Webhook page, click **Send test event** in the top right.
2. Select `checkout.session.completed` and click **Send test webhook**.
3. Open your Indic8 Dashboard. The test event will show up in your live activity stream within 2 seconds.

---

## Importing Past Sales (Optional)

If you want Indic8 to calculate MRR and sales history for past months:

1. In Stripe, go to **Developers > API keys**.
2. Under **Restricted keys**, click **Create restricted key**.
3. Set key name to `Indic8 Read-Only`.
4. Grant **Read** access to:
   - `Charges` -> Read
   - `Customer balance transactions` -> Read
   - `Disputes` -> Read
   - `Invoices` -> Read
   - `Subscriptions` -> Read
   - `Products` -> Read
5. Click **Create key** and copy the secret (`rk_live_...`).
6. Paste into Indic8 under **Historical Sync** and click **Start Import**.
