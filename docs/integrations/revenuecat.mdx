---

Connect RevenueCat to stream mobile subscription lifecycles, trial conversions, and renewals directly into Indic8.

## Quick Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your Ingress URL">
    In Indic8, go to **Settings > Integrations > RevenueCat**.
    
    Copy your endpoint URL:
    ```url
    https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/revenuecat/whk_live_your_workspace_id
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Webhook in RevenueCat">
    1. Log in to [RevenueCat](https://app.revenuecat.com).
    2. Select your Project and go to **Project Settings > Integrations > Webhooks**.
    3. Click **Add Webhook**.
    4. Set the **Webhook URL** to your Indic8 endpoint.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Authorization Header">
    In the **Authorization Header Value** field, enter the authorization token provided in your Indic8 dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Environments">
    Check **Production** and optionally **Sandbox** (sandbox events are isolated from live metrics).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Event Mapping

| RevenueCat Type | Indic8 Metric |
| :--- | :--- |
| `INITIAL_PURCHASE` | New Sale & First-Time Subscription MRR |
| `RENEWAL` | Recurring Revenue Realization |
| `CANCELLATION` | Churn Flag (Grace Period Active) |
| `EXPIRATION` | MRR Contraction & Subscription Deleted |
| `BILLING_ISSUE` | Past-Due Risk Alert |
| `PRODUCT_CHANGE` | Upgrade or Downgrade Adjustment |

<Tip>
  Indic8 automatically extracts the storefront currency (`price_in_purchased_currency`) from RevenueCat events and applies European Central Bank spot rates for accurate USD/EUR conversion.
</Tip>
