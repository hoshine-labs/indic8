Connect your iOS or macOS apps directly using Apple App Store Server Notifications (V2).

## What You Need

| Item | Where to get it | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indic8 Apple Ingress URL** | Indic8 Dashboard > Settings > Integrations > Apple | Endpoint where Apple sends signed notifications |
| **App-Specific Shared Secret** | App Store Connect > App > In-App Purchases | Validates signed transaction receipts |

---

## Step 1: Copy your Indic8 Apple URL

1. In [Indic8](https://app.indic8.ing), navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Apple App Store**.
2. Copy your unique URL:
   ```text
   https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/appstore/whk_live_xxxxxxxx
   ```

---

## Step 2: Open App Store Connect

1. Log in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com).
2. Click **My Apps** and select your application.
3. In the left menu under **General**, click **App Information**.
4. Scroll down to the section titled **App Store Server Notifications**.

---

## Step 3: Enter URL and Version

1. In **Production Server URL**, click **Set Up URL** (or **Edit**).
2. Paste the Indic8 URL you copied in Step 1.
3. Under **Version**, select **Version 2 Notifications**.
4. (Optional) Set the **Sandbox Server URL** to the same Indic8 URL to test purchases in Xcode Simulator.
5. Click **Save**.

---

## Step 4: Add Shared Secret to Indic8

1. In App Store Connect, go to **In-App Purchases > Manage**.
2. Click **App-Specific Shared Secret** and copy the 32-character key.
3. In Indic8, paste this key into the **Apple Shared Secret** field and click **Save**.

---

## Notification Types Handled

Indic8 automatically translates Apple notifications into revenue metrics:

| Notification Type | Event Subtype | What Indic8 Records |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `SUBSCRIBED` | `INITIAL_BUY` | New subscription added to MRR |
| `DID_RENEW` | -- | Subscription renewal realized |
| `DID_CHANGE_RENEWAL_STATUS` | `AUTO_RENEW_DISABLED` | Marked as scheduled cancellation |
| `EXPIRED` | `VOLUNTARY` | Subscription expired (churn recorded) |
| `REFUND` | -- | Apple customer refund deducted |
| `OFFER_REDEEMED` | `PROMOTIONAL` | Promo discount tracked |
