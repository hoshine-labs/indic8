Connect Apple App Store Server Notifications (V2) to ingest native iOS and macOS in-app purchases and subscriptions without third-party intermediaries.

## Quick Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Indic8 Webhook Ingress URL">
    In Indic8, open **Settings > Integrations > Apple App Store**.
    
    Copy your URL:
    ```url
    https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/appstore/whk_live_your_workspace_id
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure in App Store Connect">
    1. Log in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com).
    2. Select your App and go to **App Information**.
    3. Scroll down to **App Store Server Notifications**.
    4. Set **Production Server URL** to your Indic8 URL.
    5. Set **Version** to **Version 2 Notifications**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provide App Store Connect Shared Secret">
    Enter your App-Specific Shared Secret or In-App Purchase Key into Indic8 to allow cryptographic verification of Apple signed JWS payloads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Signed JWS Verification

Apple V2 notifications deliver payload contents inside a signed JSON Web Signature (JWS).

Indic8 verifies the x5c certificate chain against the Apple Root CA before decoding:
- Validates the timestamp and bundle identifier.
- Extracts `transactionId`, `originalTransactionId`, and `productIdentifier`.
- Evaluates promotional offer redemptions and renewal states.
