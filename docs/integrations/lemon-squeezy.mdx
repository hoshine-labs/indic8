Connect Lemon Squeezy to aggregate Merchant of Record sales, EU VAT calculations, license key activations, and recurring billing.

## Quick Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Indic8 Webhook Ingress URL">
    In your Indic8 dashboard, go to **Settings > Integrations > Lemon Squeezy**.
    
    Copy your endpoint URL:
    ```url
    https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/lemonsqueezy/whk_live_your_workspace_id
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Webhook in Lemon Squeezy">
    1. Log in to your [Lemon Squeezy Dashboard](https://app.lemonsqueezy.com).
    2. Go to **Settings > Webhooks**.
    3. Click the **+** button to create a new webhook.
    4. Set the **Callback URL** to your Indic8 endpoint.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Signing Secret">
    Enter a strong secret string under **Signing secret**, and paste the identical string into your Indic8 Lemon Squeezy integration settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Events">
    Select the following webhook topics:
    
    - `order_created`
    - `order_refunded`
    - `subscription_created`
    - `subscription_updated`
    - `subscription_cancelled`
    - `subscription_resumed`
    - `subscription_expired`
    - `license_key_created`
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tax and MoR Fee Reconciliation

Because Lemon Squeezy acts as a Merchant of Record, orders include customer-paid sales tax and platform processing fees.

Indic8 automatically breaks down every order:
- **Total Customer Paid**: Full amount charged.
- **Taxes & VAT**: Separated from your company net revenue.
- **Lemon Squeezy Fee**: Logged as gateway processing expense.
- **Your Net Earnings**: Added to real-time cash flow metrics.
