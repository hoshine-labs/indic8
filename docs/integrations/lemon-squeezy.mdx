---

Connect Lemon Squeezy to track your product orders, tax breakdowns, license key activations, and subscription renewals.

## What You Need

| Item | Where to get it | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indic8 Webhook URL** | Indic8 Dashboard > Settings > Integrations > Lemon Squeezy | Destination for Lemon Squeezy notifications |
| **Signing Secret** | You create this string in Lemon Squeezy | Secures webhook delivery with cryptographic signatures |

---

## Step 1: Copy your Indic8 Webhook URL

1. In [Indic8](https://app.indic8.ing), go to **Settings > Integrations > Lemon Squeezy**.
2. Click **Copy** next to your Webhook URL:
   ```text
   https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/lemonsqueezy/whk_live_xxxxxxxx
   ```

---

## Step 2: Add Webhook in Lemon Squeezy

1. Open your [Lemon Squeezy Dashboard](https://app.lemonsqueezy.com).
2. In the left navigation, click **Settings**.
3. Under the **Developers** section, click **Webhooks**.
4. Click the purple **+** button (or **Create Webhook**).
5. In **Callback URL**, paste the URL from Step 1.
6. In **Secret**, type a secure random string (for example, generate one using a password manager).
7. Copy this secret to your clipboard.

---

## Step 3: Select Webhook Events

Check the boxes for these 8 topics:

| Event Topic | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| `order_created` | New product checkouts and one-time orders |
| `order_refunded` | Customer refunds and charge deductions |
| `subscription_created` | New recurring subscription checkouts |
| `subscription_updated` | Plan updates, renewals, and card changes |
| `subscription_cancelled` | Canceled subscriptions |
| `subscription_resumed` | Re-activated subscriptions |
| `subscription_expired` | Expired subscriptions (churn) |
| `license_key_created` | Issued software license keys |

Click **Create Webhook**.

---

## Step 4: Paste Secret in Indic8 & Save

1. Go back to your Indic8 Lemon Squeezy settings tab.
2. Paste the exact secret string you created in Step 2 into the **Signing Secret** box.
3. Click **Save & Connect**.

---

## Step 5: Test the Integration

1. In Lemon Squeezy, on the webhook details page, click the **...** menu on the right.
2. Click **Send test**.
3. Select `order_created` and click **Send**.
4. Check your Indic8 dashboard to confirm the test order arrives with sub-2s latency.
