title: "Paddle Billing Integration"
description: "Sync Paddle Billing checkouts, recurring billing, customer tax deductions, and adjustments."
---

Connect Paddle Billing to capture software license transactions, B2B invoicing, and multi-region tax settlements.

## Quick Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Retrieve your Webhook Destination URL">
    In the Indic8 dashboard, go to **Settings > Integrations > Paddle**.
    
    Copy your URL:
    ```url
    https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/paddle/whk_live_your_workspace_id
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Notification Destination in Paddle">
    1. Log in to [Paddle Billing](https://vendors.paddle.com).
    2. Go to **Developer Tools > Notifications**.
    3. Click **New destination**.
    4. Select **Webhook** and paste your Indic8 URL.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Subscribe to Events">
    Select these event types:
    - `transaction.completed`
    - `transaction.billed`
    - `transaction.canceled`
    - `subscription.created`
    - `subscription.updated`
    - `subscription.canceled`
    - `adjustment.created`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and Copy Secret">
    Copy the **Notification Secret Key** (`pdl_ntf_set_...`) from Paddle and save it inside your Indic8 settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>
