Connect Paddle Billing to track software sales, B2B recurring invoicing, and tax withholdings.

## What You Need

| Item | Where to get it | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indic8 Destination URL** | Indic8 Dashboard > Settings > Integrations > Paddle | The endpoint for Paddle notifications |
| **Notification Secret Key** | Paddle Dashboard > Developer Tools > Notifications | Verifies webhook authenticity |

---

## Step 1: Copy your Indic8 Paddle URL

1. In [Indic8](https://app.indic8.ing), go to **Settings > Integrations > Paddle**.
2. Click **Copy** next to your Webhook URL:
   ```text
   https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/paddle/whk_live_xxxxxxxx
   ```

---

## Step 2: Add Destination in Paddle

1. Log in to your [Paddle Dashboard](https://vendors.paddle.com).
2. In the left navigation, click **Developer tools**.
3. Click **Notifications** in the submenu.
4. Click the blue button **+ New destination**.
5. Set **Destination name** to `Indic8 Revenue Telemetry`.
6. Set **Notification type** to **Webhook**.
7. In **Endpoint URL**, paste the URL from Step 1.

---

## Step 3: Select Required Events

Check the boxes for these 7 events:

| Event Name | What it updates in Indic8 |
| :--- | :--- |
| `transaction.completed` | Successful customer payments and checkouts |
| `transaction.billed` | Invoiced B2B orders |
| `transaction.canceled` | Canceled orders |
| `subscription.created` | New active recurring subscriptions |
| `subscription.updated` | Plan upgrades, downgrades, and price renewals |
| `subscription.canceled` | Canceled subscriptions (tracked as churn) |
| `adjustment.created` | Refunds, chargebacks, and credit adjustments |

Click **Save destination**.

---

## Step 4: Copy Secret into Indic8

1. In Paddle, under your newly created notification destination, find the **Secret key** (starts with `pdl_ntf_set_...`).
2. Click **Copy**.
3. In Indic8, paste the key into the **Paddle Secret** field and click **Save & Connect**.

---

## Step 5: Verify Setup

1. In Paddle, click **Send test notification**.
2. Select `transaction.completed` and click **Send**.
3. Check your Indic8 dashboard to confirm the test transaction appears.
