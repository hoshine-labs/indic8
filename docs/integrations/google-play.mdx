title: "Google Play Setup Guide"
description: "Connect Android in-app purchases and subscriptions using Google Cloud Pub/Sub."
---

Connect Google Play Real-Time Developer Notifications (RTDN) to ingest Android in-app purchases and recurring subscriptions.

## What You Need

| Item | Where to get it | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indic8 Google Ingress URL** | Indic8 Dashboard > Settings > Integrations > Google Play | The push endpoint for Google Cloud Pub/Sub |
| **Google Cloud Pub/Sub Topic** | Google Cloud Console | Receives real-time events from Google Play |

---

## Step 1: Create a Google Cloud Pub/Sub Topic

1. Open the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com).
2. Ensure you have selected the project linked to your Google Play Console.
3. In the search bar, type `Pub/Sub` and open **Topics**.
4. Click **Create Topic**.
5. Set Topic ID to: `indic8-rtdn-topic`.
6. Click **Create**.

---

## Step 2: Grant Google Play Permission to Publish

1. On your newly created topic page, click the **Permissions** tab on the right side.
2. Click **Add Principal**.
3. In **New principals**, enter:
   ```text
   google-play-developer-notifications@system.gserviceaccount.com
   ```
4. In **Select a role**, choose **Pub/Sub Publisher**.
5. Click **Save**.

---

## Step 3: Create a Push Subscription

1. In Pub/Sub, go to **Subscriptions** in the left menu.
2. Click **Create Subscription**.
3. Set Subscription ID to: `indic8-rtdn-sub`.
4. Select the topic: `projects/your-project/topics/indic8-rtdn-topic`.
5. Under **Delivery type**, select **Push**.
6. In **Endpoint URL**, paste the URL from Indic8 (**Settings > Integrations > Google Play**).
7. Leave all other settings as default and click **Create**.

---

## Step 4: Link Topic in Google Play Console

1. Open the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Select your Android App.
3. In the left menu, scroll down to **Monetize > Monetization setup**.
4. Scroll to **Real-time developer notifications**.
5. In **Topic name**, enter your full Pub/Sub path:
   ```text
   projects/your-gcp-project-id/topics/indic8-rtdn-topic
   ```
6. Click **Send test notification** to verify the connection.
7. Click **Save changes** at the bottom of the screen.
