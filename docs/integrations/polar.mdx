Connect Polar to track developer sponsorships, software file sales, and subscription tiers.

## What You Need

| Item | Where to get it | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Indic8 Webhook URL** | Indic8 Dashboard > Settings > Integrations > Polar | The destination URL for Polar events |
| **Polar Webhook Secret** | Polar Dashboard > Settings > Webhooks | Verifies payload signature authenticity |

---

## Step 1: Copy your Indic8 Polar URL

1. In your [Indic8 Dashboard](https://app.indic8.ing), click **Settings**.
2. Click **Integrations**, then click **Polar**.
3. Copy your unique Polar webhook URL:
   ```text
   https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/polar/whk_live_xxxxxxxx
   ```

---

## Step 2: Create Webhook in Polar.sh

1. Log in to [Polar.sh](https://polar.sh/dashboard).
2. Select your organization or personal profile from the top left dropdown.
3. In the left sidebar, click **Settings**.
4. Scroll down to the **Webhooks** section and click **Add Endpoint**.
5. Paste your Indic8 URL into the **Endpoint URL** field.
6. Under **Format**, keep the default `JSON`.

---

## Step 3: Select Polar Events

Check the boxes for these 6 events:

| Event Name | What it updates in Indic8 |
| :--- | :--- |
| `order.created` | One-time software purchases, product sales, and donations |
| `order.refunded` | Customer refunds and charge deductions |
| `subscription.created` | New recurring tier backers and subscription signups |
| `subscription.updated` | Tier upgrades, tier downgrades, and price updates |
| `subscription.canceled` | Canceled pledges (tracked as churn) |
| `benefit_grant.created` | License key and GitHub benefit grants |

Click **Save Endpoint**.

---

## Step 4: Copy Secret into Indic8

1. In your newly created Polar webhook page, click **Show Secret** to reveal the secret string.
2. Copy this value.
3. Return to Indic8 and paste it into the **Polar Secret** field.
4. Click **Save & Connect**.

---

## Step 5: Test Connection

1. On the Polar webhook details page, click **Send Test Event**.
2. Pick `order.created` and click **Send**.
3. Check your Indic8 overview page. You will see the event appear immediately in the live feed.
