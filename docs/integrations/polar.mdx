Connect Polar to track open-source sponsorship tiers, digital file downloads, and software license revenue in real time.

## Quick Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Indic8 Webhook Ingress URL">
    Navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Polar** in your Indic8 dashboard.
    
    Copy your endpoint URL:
    ```url
    https://api.indic8.ing/v1/webhooks/polar/whk_live_your_workspace_id
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Webhook in Polar">
    1. Log in to your [Polar.sh Dashboard](https://polar.sh/dashboard).
    2. Navigate to **Settings > Webhooks**.
    3. Click **Add Webhook Endpoint**.
    4. Paste your Indic8 Webhook URL.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Subscribe to Events">
    Enable the following event triggers:
    
    - `order.created`
    - `order.refunded`
    - `subscription.created`
    - `subscription.updated`
    - `subscription.canceled`
    - `benefit_grant.created`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste Secret Token">
    Copy the generated **Webhook Secret** from Polar into Indic8 and click **Connect Polar**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Supported Polar Entities

- **Digital Products**: Individual one-off software licenses or downloads.
- **Monthly Pledges & Tiers**: Recurring developer subscriptions and sponsorships.
- **Donations & Tips**: Non-recurring support contributions.

```json
{
  "event": "order.created",
  "data": {
    "id": "ord_polar_90214",
    "amount": 2900,
    "currency": "usd",
    "product_id": "prod_polar_cli",
    "customer": {
      "email": "dev@example.com"
    }
  }
}
```

<Note>
  Indic8 normalizes Polar products automatically into your product catalog and matches them against existing Unified Products by SKU slug.
</Note>
